NPO accountants at Turning Point Chartered Accountants SA work only in this sector — monthly books, project ledgers, annual financial statements and board reporting that match how South African NPOs actually operate.

Whether you are a community organisation preparing for your first donor audit, or a PBO that needs clean management accounts every month, we set up the numbers so compliance work sits on a solid base. Accounting here is not a side desk. It is the same practice that already supports NPO audit, VAT and tax under one roof.

Frequently Asked Questions About NPO Accountants

What do NPO accountants do differently from normal accountants?

NPO accountants track restricted donor funds, project codes and board reporting the way non-profits are governed — not like a trading company. They prepare packs that donors, the NPO Directorate and auditors recognise, and they flag VAT or PBO issues early instead of leaving them for year-end.

Do we need NPO accountants if we already use Xero?

Software does not replace judgement. We often take over or tidy Xero charts of accounts so funds, grants and payroll post correctly. You keep the cloud tools; we make the structure fit an NPO.

Can you prepare annual financial statements for an NPO?

Yes. We prepare AFS suited to your entity type and reporting framework, ready for audit or independent review. Clean AFS are one of the highest-value outputs of good NPO accounting.

How do you handle restricted donor funds?

We set project or fund ledgers, track income against donor conditions, and report remaining balances clearly for boards and funders. That is the difference between “we spent the grant” and “we can prove it.”

Will your accounting work with our NPO auditor?

Yes. We prepare working papers and reconciliations that auditors expect. If TPCSA is also your auditor, independence rules are respected — accounting and audit stay properly separated inside the firm’s processes.

Do you support payroll for NPOs?

We support payroll accounting and related reconciliations, and we coordinate with tax when EMP201 or IRP5 issues appear. Payroll is part of the monthly close, not an afterthought.

Can you help with DSD and board reporting packs?

We build management accounts and board packs on a monthly or quarterly rhythm, and we support the financial side of NPO Directorate reporting so figures match the books.

What size NPO do you work with?

From smaller registered organisations that need a reliable monthly close, through to donor-funded programmes with multiple projects. If you are unsure, the Needs Analysis shows what level of support fits.

How do NPO accountants work with VAT and PBO compliance?

Numbers and tax status talk to each other. Our accountants raise VAT and PBO questions early; specialists then handle registration, historical VAT reviews or PBO applications where needed — without you briefing a second firm from zero.
