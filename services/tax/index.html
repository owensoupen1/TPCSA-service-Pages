Frequently Asked Questions About NPO Tax

Do NPOs pay tax in South Africa?

Not automatically. Registering as an NPO does not by itself exempt you from tax. Organisations with PBO approval can be exempt on qualifying receipts, but trading income and non-qualifying activities can still be taxed. We review your position first.

What is the difference between an NPO and a PBO for tax?

An NPO is a registration under the Nonprofit Organisations Act. A PBO is a tax status granted by SARS. The tax benefits come from PBO approval and ongoing compliance, not from NPO registration alone.

Is trading income taxed at 27%?

Trading income that falls outside the PBO exemption limits can be taxed at the company rate, currently 27%. Whether and how much applies depends on your activities and the partial taxation rules, so we review each income stream.

What is IT3(EI) and do we need it?

IT3(EI) is a SARS return that PBOs and other exempt organisations may need to submit for amounts received and accrued. We help confirm whether it applies to you and prepare the information. If you issue Section 18A certificates, the separate IT3(d) return may also apply; 10BucksOnly IT3(d) is a separate software product for IT3(d) filing automation.

How is Section 18A different from income tax exemption?

Income tax exemption relates to your own tax position as a PBO. Section 18A lets donors claim a deduction for qualifying donations to you. They are linked, but they are separate approvals with separate duties.

When should we apply for PBO status?

As soon as your constitution and activities qualify, ideally before you start receiving significant funding. See our guide to PBO and Section 18A approval for the process.

What documents do you need for NPO tax work?

Usually your NPO registration, constitution or MOI, any PBO letter, annual financial statements, recent tax returns, SARS correspondence and details of your income sources. We give you a checklist at the start.

How is tax work different from VAT work?

Tax work covers income tax, PBO exemption, partial taxation and returns. VAT is a separate tax with its own registration and rules. See our VAT services for NPOs for that side.

From small organisations with one funder to larger donor-funded programmes. If you are unsure what level of support you need, a Needs Analysis will show you.
