Looking for NPO VAT Help in South Africa?

Being a non-profit does not give you an automatic VAT answer. Some organisations must register, some should not, and some can claim input tax only in part. NPO VAT depends on what you supply, how you are funded and whether your welfare activities or association-not-for-gain status brings special treatment.

We look at the full picture: your activities, funding agreements, books and SARS history. Our VAT work sits next to our NPO accountants and tax services for NPOs, so the numbers, the tax position and the returns all tell the same story.

Frequently Asked Questions About NPO VAT

Can an NPO claim VAT?

Yes, if the NPO is registered for VAT and the expenses relate to taxable supplies, it can generally claim input tax with valid tax invoices. An NPO is not automatically exempt or automatically entitled to claims — the answer depends on what the organisation supplies and how it is funded.

Do all NPOs register for VAT?

No. Registration is compulsory only when taxable supplies exceed the threshold, or voluntary if you qualify and it makes commercial sense. Many NPOs do not need to register, and some should not. We review your activities and income before advising.

What is welfare organisation VAT registration?

Certain welfare and public benefit organisations may qualify for special VAT treatment on specific supplies and donations received. We assess whether your activities and constitution meet the requirements, then guide the registration and ongoing treatment.

What is VAT apportionment?

Apportionment is how an organisation splits input tax when it makes both taxable and non-taxable supplies, for example grant-funded activities alongside fee-based services. A sound method protects valid claims and reduces SARS risk.

How far back can we claim VAT?

Claims are generally limited by SARS time limits and your registration history, so older periods can be lost. A historical VAT review shows what may still be recoverable and what supporting documents are needed.

How do you work with historical VAT reviews?

Our VAT team reviews past returns, invoices and funding agreements, quantifies possible refunds or exposure, and then supports the correction or claim with SARS. See our historical VAT reviews page for the full process.

Can you help with VAT201 returns?

Yes. We support VAT201 preparation and review, reconcile VAT to the ledger, and help you submit on time. Where the books show errors, we work with your accountants to fix the source.

What documents do we need for NPO VAT work?

Typically your NPO registration, constitution or MOI, VAT registration details, recent VAT201s, tax invoices, funding or grant agreements, bank statements and the general ledger. We give you a short checklist at the start.

What size NPO do you work with?

From small registered organisations with a single funder to larger donor-funded programmes with several projects. If you are unsure what level of VAT support fits, the Needs Analysis will show you.
