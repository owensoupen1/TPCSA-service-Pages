Frequently Asked Questions About Audit Services for NPOs

If financial reporting is mandatory, does that mean every NPO needs a full audit?

No. Every registered NPO has financial reporting responsibilities, but not every NPO automatically needs a full statutory audit. Depending on your structure, founding documents, funder requirements or company obligations, you may need an audit, independent review, accounting officer’s report, donor audit or fund audit.

What happens if our records are not audit-ready?

The audit process can become slower, more disruptive and more difficult for your team. Missing documents, unclear allocations or incomplete explanations usually create more back-and-forth, which is why audit readiness support can help before the formal process begins.

Can you help us understand which type of audit or review we actually need?

Yes. Many NPOs know they need “something” for reporting, funders or compliance, but are not always sure whether that means a statutory audit, donor audit, fund audit, independent review or accounting officer’s report. We help clarify what applies before the work begins.

Do funders usually ask for the same type of audit?

No. Different funders may ask for different levels of reporting, review or audit work. Some may require project-specific reporting, some may require a donor audit, and others may require audited annual financial statements or supporting schedules.

Can audit support reduce disruption for our team?

Yes. A well-managed audit process should not feel like your team is constantly being pulled away from daily work. Clear communication, organised records and proper preparation can reduce unnecessary pressure and help the process move more efficiently.

Can an audit help our NPO attract higher-level donors or funders?

Yes. Strong audit records can help show that your NPO manages funds responsibly, keeps proper financial records and takes accountability seriously. This can make your organisation more credible when engaging with larger donors, funders or partners who need confidence before committing support.

What does an NPO auditor do?

An NPO auditor examines an organisation’s financial statements, records and controls to provide an independent audit opinion.

When does an NPO need an audit?

An NPO financial audit may be required by law, funders, governance requirements, agreements or an organisation’s own policies. Read this Quick Guide to understand whether your NPO actually needs an audit.

What are NPO audit services?

NPO audit services cover the audit process, including planning, financial statement review, supporting evidence and the final audit report.

How do I choose an NPO external auditor?

Choose an NPO external auditor with relevant non-profit experience and an understanding of financial reporting, compliance and funder requirements.
