Frequently Asked Questions About Xero for NPOs

Is Xero suitable for a South African NPO?

Yes, for most small and medium NPOs. Xero handles South African bank feeds, VAT settings and multi-user access. What makes it work for an NPO is the setup: a chart of accounts and tracking categories built for funds and projects.

How do you track donor funds in Xero?

We usually use tracking categories for funds or projects, combined with account codes for restricted and unrestricted income. Reports can then be filtered per funder, so you can show what each grant received and spent.

We are new to Xero. What does onboarding involve?

We agree the structure, create the file, load opening balances from your last approved figures, connect bank feeds and train your team. Timing depends on how clean the existing records are.

We already use Xero but the reports are a mess. Can you help?

Yes. We review the file, fix miscoded transactions and unreconciled items, and restructure tracking where needed. Historical clean-up is scoped first so you know what is involved.

Does Xero have special pricing for non-profits?

Xero has offered discounts to eligible non-profits in some regions. Check current eligibility and pricing directly with Xero, as offers change.

Can Xero produce our annual financial statements?

Xero holds the data, but annual financial statements still need to be prepared and reviewed under the right framework. We use the Xero data to prepare AFS ready for your auditor or reviewer.

Can payroll and other apps connect to Xero?

Many payroll and expense apps integrate with Xero. We help choose and connect tools that suit your size, and make sure payroll journals post to the right funds.

Will our auditor accept Xero records?

Auditors commonly work with Xero. What matters is reconciled accounts, supporting documents and a clear trail per fund. Our setup is designed with that in mind.

Do you provide ongoing Xero support?

Yes. Some organisations want a one-off setup and training; others keep us for monthly reviews or full bookkeeping through our NPO accountants.
