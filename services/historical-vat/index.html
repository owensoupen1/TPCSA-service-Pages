Frequently Asked Questions About Historical VAT

How far back can a historical VAT review go?

SARS time limits and your VAT registration history decide how far back a claim or correction can go. Older periods can be lost, so the sooner we review, the more may be recoverable. We confirm the open periods at the start.

Which NPOs qualify for a historical VAT review?

Any VAT-registered NPO, PBO or welfare organisation that suspects it under-claimed, over-claimed or used the wrong VAT treatment in past periods. Organisations that registered late, or changed activities, are common candidates.

Is the review different for an NPO and a PBO?

The process is the same, but the rules that apply can differ. A PBO or welfare organisation may have special treatment on certain supplies and donations, while other NPOs follow the standard rules. We apply the correct treatment to each period.

What documents do we need?

Usually your VAT registration details, filed VAT201s, tax invoices, funding or grant agreements, bank statements and the general ledger. We give you a short checklist so you only gather what is needed.

Can you guarantee a refund?

No. A refund depends on what the records show and what SARS accepts. We tell you honestly what looks recoverable, what is at risk and what documents are missing before you decide to proceed.

How is this different from a normal VAT review?

A normal review looks at current returns and processes. A historical VAT review goes back through closed periods, recalculates claims and apportionment, and prepares corrections or claims to SARS.

How long does a historical VAT review take?

It depends on the number of periods and the state of your records. Most reviews take a few weeks once documents are received. We give you a timeline after the first look at your files.

Does the board need to approve the claim?

We recommend it. Corrections and claims can change your VAT position, so we prepare a short board briefing pack explaining the findings, the risks and the recommended action.

What happens after the review?

You can move to ongoing support so the same errors do not repeat. See our VAT services for NPOs for monthly VAT201 and advisory support.
